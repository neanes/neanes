# Neume Keyboard

<style>
.neume {
    font-family: Neanes;
    font-size: 20pt;
}
</style>

Most common neumes have a keyboard shortcut. The layout is designed so that your right hand enters neumes while your left hand selects a category or variation.

By default, the right-hand keys enter characters of quantity such as Ison, Oligon, and Apostrophos. Hold a modifier key with your left hand to use those same right-hand keys for fthoras, gorgons, signs of quality, and other categories.

For example, press <kbd>J</kbd> for Ison or <kbd>K</kbd> for Oligon. Hold <kbd>T</kbd> and press <kbd>J</kbd> for the Diatonic Ni Low fthora, or hold <kbd>T</kbd> and press <kbd>K</kbd> for the Diatonic Pa fthora.

Shortcuts use the physical key positions of a US keyboard layout. In the tables below, a plus sign means to hold the first key or keys while pressing the last key.
