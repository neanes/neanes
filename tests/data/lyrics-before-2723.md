# Historical lyrics compatibility fixture

`lyrics-before-2723.byzx` contains note assignments generated by the unmodified
`LyricService.ts` and `MelismaHelperGreek.ts` from commit
`273bf2dd27f632cf654fbc13187383f0b06207bd`, immediately before #2723.

The six notes are Apostrophos, Hyporoe, Ison, KentemataPlusOligon, Ison, Ison.
All have `acceptsLyrics: Default`. Lyrics are locked, with Greek melismas enabled
and RTL disabled. The input is the fixture's `staff.lyrics.text`.

The historical service assigned lyrics to initially empty `NoteElement` objects
using its `updateNote` callback. `SaveService.SaveScoreToJson` serialized those
assignments. The fixture therefore tests actual historical Greek splitting, rather
than approximating it by changing defaults in today's assignment engine.

The regression must retain the saved assignments during loading. A later edit
may use today's equivalent shared-pair Greek rendering, but the four following
syllables must keep their positions through edits and save/load round trips.
